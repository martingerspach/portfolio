Questions teams ask first

How is this different from Zapier or n8n?
Those tools run the fixed steps you draw. Weft writes the steps from your description, adjusts them when an input changes and asks when it is unsure. You can still lock any step.
Which AI models does it use?
You choose per job: Claude, GPT or Gemini through us, or your own keys and open models. Changing the model keeps the job, its approvals and its skills.
Where does our data go?
Weft runs in our EU or US region, or inside your own cloud on Enterprise. Your data never trains a model, and every action stays in a log you can export.
What happens when a run fails?
It retries the steps that are safe to retry. If that fails too, it stops and posts what it tried and where it stopped, so a person can pick it up.
